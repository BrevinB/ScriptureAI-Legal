# Privacy Policy for ScriptureAI

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026

This policy covers the ScriptureAI apps for iPhone and iPad (iOS) and for Android.

## Overview

ScriptureAI is designed with privacy in mind. There are no accounts, your studies stay on your device, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Information We Collect

### Data You Create
- **Studies and questions**: Your questions, the AI's answers, and your conversation history
- **Notes, prayers, devotionals, and bookmarks**: Anything you write or save in the app, including photos or files you attach to notes
- **Preferences**: Your Bible translation, tradition, and other app settings

All of this is stored on your device. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

### Technical Data
- **Install ID**: A random identifier created when you install the app. It is sent with AI requests so we can apply rate limits that keep the service available. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account.
- **Purchase information**: If you subscribe, your app store and our subscription provider (see below) process your purchase and subscription status.
- **Usage analytics (iOS only)**: The iOS app sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, such as "a study was started" or "the paywall was shown." TelemetryDeck anonymizes identifiers on your device, and these signals never include your questions, notes, or other content. The Android app does not send usage analytics.

## How We Use Your Information

- **To answer your questions**: When you ask a question, generate a devotional, or request Scripture suggestions, the text needed for that request is sent to an AI service to produce the response
- **To keep the service available**: The install ID is used only for rate limiting
- **To provide subscriptions**: Purchase information is used to unlock ScriptureAI Pro and restore it on reinstall
- **To improve the app (iOS only)**: Anonymous usage signals show which features are used

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share data only with the services needed to run the app:

### AI Processing (OpenAI)
- Your questions and the context needed to answer them (such as earlier messages in that study, your translation, and your tradition) are sent through our server to OpenAI to generate a response
- Our server passes the request along and does not store your questions or the answers
- OpenAI's privacy policy applies to data it processes. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train its models

### Hosting (Cloudflare)
- Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver requests and apply rate limits

### Subscriptions (Apple, Google, and RevenueCat)
- Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play and are subject to their privacy policies
- We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID, never your name or email from us

### Analytics (TelemetryDeck, iOS only)
- Anonymous usage signals as described above

### No Advertising or Tracking
- We do not show ads or use your data for advertising
- We do not sell your personal information
- We do not track you across other apps or websites

## Data Storage and Backup

### On Your Device
- Your studies, notes, prayers, and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device

### iCloud Sync (iOS, Optional)
- If you turn on iCloud Sync, your data syncs through your private iCloud account using Apple's encrypted infrastructure
- We cannot access your iCloud data

### Android Backup
- If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include ScriptureAI's data (but not note attachments) in your Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. Google encrypts this backup, and we cannot access it
- You can turn backup off in your device's settings

## Your Choices and Controls

- **View your data**: Everything you create is visible in the app
- **Delete your data**: Delete individual studies, notes, prayers, and devotionals in the app. On iOS, Settings also lets you delete all chats. On Android, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of its data from your device
- **No account**: There is no account to create or delete
- **Subscriptions**: Manage or cancel through the App Store or Google Play

## Children's Privacy

ScriptureAI does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for general Bible study and is suitable for all ages with appropriate supervision.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version, and we will tell you about significant changes in the app or by other appropriate means.
