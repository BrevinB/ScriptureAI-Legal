## Common Questions

### Subscriptions & Purchases (iPhone and iPad)
- **Restore Purchases**: Open Settings → Usage & Billing → Restore Purchases
- **Cancel Subscription**: Manage through your [Apple ID Subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)
- **Refund Request**: Contact Apple Support or use their automated refund system

### Subscriptions & Purchases (Android)
- **Restore Purchases**: Open Settings → ScriptureAI Pro → Restore Purchases
- **Cancel Subscription**: Open Settings → Manage Subscription, or manage it in [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
- **Refund Request**: Request one through [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)
- **Switching phones**: Subscriptions don't transfer between iPhone and Android, because ScriptureAI has no accounts

### Backups on Android
- Android backs up your studies, notes, and prayers to your Google account if backup is on for your device, and restores them when you set up a new phone
- Photos and files attached to notes move only with a direct phone-to-phone transfer

### iCloud Sync Issues
- **Enable Sync**: Settings → Data & Sync → Toggle "iCloud Sync"
- **Sync Problems**: Try turning iCloud Sync off and back on
- **Missing Conversations**: Ensure you're signed into the same iCloud account

### App Issues
- **Reset App**: Try force-closing and reopening the app
- **Clear Cache**: Delete and reinstall the app (your iCloud data is safe)
- **iOS Compatibility**: ScriptureAI requires iOS 17.6 or later
- **Android Compatibility**: ScriptureAI requires Android 8.0 or later

### Message Limits
- **Free Tier**: 15 messages to try ScriptureAI
- **Check Usage**: Settings → Usage & Billing to see your current usage
- **Upgrade Options**: Various subscription and one-time purchase options available
